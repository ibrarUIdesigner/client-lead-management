# Offer Strategy Playbook

Value proposition positioning, pricing brackets, and industry archetype messaging.

## 1. Offer Pricing Brackets ($500 – $1,000 USD)

To build confidence and eliminate ambiguous agency estimates, all offers use settled, transparent pricing:

| Offer Package | Price | Scope & Positioning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Homepage Refresh** | **$550** | Visual modernization, typography & color cleanup, primary CTA button in hero, mobile spacing fixes. |
| **Friction Repair & UX** | **$750** | Fixes 2–3 verified audit blockers (e.g. mobile overflow, slow LCP image optimization, appointment integration, quote form). |
| **Comprehensive Redesign** | **$950** | Full multi-section homepage redesign with curated Unsplash/Pixabay imagery, customer review showcase, service hierarchy, and conversion funnels. |
| **Brand New Site** | **$850** | For businesses with no website. Complete modern responsive single-page site with domain setup guidance, contact forms, and local SEO structure. |

## 2. Archetype Value Propositions

### 1. High-Trust Services (Dental, Medical, Legal)
- **Primary Goal**: Trust, appointment booking, physician/lawyer credibility.
- **Key Offer Elements**: Prominent accreditation/review badge, streamlined online consultation form, clear patient/client next steps.

### 2. Local Emergency & Trades (Roofing, HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical)
- **Primary Goal**: Immediate inbound phone calls and emergency dispatch quotes.
- **Key Offer Elements**: Click-to-call mobile header, 24/7 service area badges, 3-question estimate form.

### 3. Visual & Aesthetic (Salons, Spas, Interior Design, Fine Dining)
- **Primary Goal**: Sensory appeal, ambiance, portfolio showcase, reservation request.
- **Key Offer Elements**: High-resolution curated photography, before & after galleries, simple booking flow.

### 4. Professional B2B (Accounting, Consulting, Corporate Services)
- **Primary Goal**: Authority, case studies, discovery consultation request.
- **Key Offer Elements**: Process breakdown, client testimonials, clear service packages.
