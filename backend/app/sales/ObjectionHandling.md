# Objection Handling Playbook

Structured rules for classifying prospect objections and drafting empathetic, high-converting responses.

## 1. Golden Rules of Objection Handling
1. **Never Argue or Get Defensive**: Acknowledge their point immediately.
2. **Anchor to Value, Not Features**: Focus on captured appointments, mobile inquiries, and customer trust.
3. **Keep Replies Under 80 Words**: Long explanations look insecure.
4. **Always Require Human Approval**: Suggested responses must be reviewed by the user before dispatch.

---

## 2. Common Scenarios & Suggested Responses

### Objection 1: "How much does this cost?" (Pricing Question)
- **Classification**: `Pricing`
- **Objective**: Be transparent with fixed pricing brackets; offer a quick walkthrough of the concept.
- **Draft Response**:
  ```text
  Hi {{first_name|there}},

  A straightforward homepage refresh with mobile optimization and appointment integration is a flat $750 (or $850 for a full new build), with no recurring designer retainer.

  I already have the concept laid out for {{business_name}}. Would you have 10 minutes this Thursday or Friday for a quick screen-share to see if it fits what you need?
  ```

---

### Objection 2: "We already have someone / an agency who handles our site."
- **Classification**: `Objection`
- **Objective**: Compliment their partner, point out that you can share the specific mobile/speed audit findings directly with them or their team with zero pressure.
- **Draft Response**:
  ```text
  Hi {{first_name|there}},

  Totally understand and glad to hear you have someone in your corner.

  The mobile friction I noticed was mainly around the 4-second content delay and the button positioning. I'd be happy to send over the audit notes free of charge so your web team can patch it up directly.

  Would that be helpful?
  ```

---

### Objection 3: "Not right now / Reach out next quarter."
- **Classification**: `Not Interested` or `Nurture`
- **Objective**: Respect timing, provide a non-intrusive link to view the mockup on their own time, and set a calendar reminder.
- **Draft Response**:
  ```text
  Understood, {{first_name|there}} — thanks for letting me know.

  I'll leave the mockup preview live here: {{mockup_url}} whenever you have a free minute to glance at it.

  I'll touch base in a couple of months to see how things are progressing. Wishing you a strong quarter!
  ```

---

### Objection 4: "Is this an automated pitch / spam?"
- **Classification**: `Question` / `Objection`
- **Objective**: Demonstrate authentic, human research with a hyper-specific observation.
- **Draft Response**:
  ```text
  Fair question! No automated spam here — I'm an independent web designer and personally audited {{business_name}}'s site after seeing your great reviews in {{city}}.

  I specifically designed the concept around your {{featured_service}} because that's where most new inquiries originate. 

  Happy to share the design link so you can judge the work for yourself.
  ```

---

### Objection 5: "Sounds interesting, send me more details."
- **Classification**: `Interested` / `Question`
- **Objective**: Send the mockup link immediately with 2 bullet points of what was improved, and offer a short call.
- **Draft Response**:
  ```text
  Glad you're open to taking a look!

  Here is the interactive preview I prepared for {{business_name}}:
  {{mockup_url}}

  Key improvements in this concept:
  1. Instant mobile booking CTA sticky in header.
  2. Prominent showcase for your 5-star customer reviews.

  Take a look and let me know your thoughts. If you'd like to adjust any sections or discuss next steps, I'm happy to hop on a quick 10-minute call.
  ```
