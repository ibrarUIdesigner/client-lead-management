# Lead Qualification & Scoring Playbook

Objective framework for evaluating prospect fit, calculating explainable opportunity scores, and prioritizing outreach.

## 1. Scoring Principles

- **No Black-Box Numbers**: Never output an unexplained AI number. Every Opportunity Score (0–100) must be a verifiable sum of distinct additive factors.
- **Explainability Requirement**: When displaying a score (e.g. 87/100), always provide the breakdown factors (e.g., `+20 Website Weakness`, `+18 High-Ticket Industry`, `+15 Verified Friction Evidence`).

---

## 2. Score Breakdown Dimensions (Max 100 Points)

### Dimension 1: Website Opportunity (0 to 25 Points)
- **No Website**: +25 points (Prime candidate for a new site build).
- **Critical Technical Friction** (Overall audit < 50, LCP > 4s, or mobile overflow): +20 points.
- **Moderate Friction** (Overall audit 50–70, missing CTA, or missing contact form): +15 points.
- **Minor Polish Needed** (Audit 70–85): +8 points.
- **Modern / Healthy Site** (Audit > 85): 0 points.

### Dimension 2: Business & Industry Fit (0 to 25 Points)
- **High-Ticket Local Services** (Dental, Medical, Legal, Roofing, HVAC, Construction, Luxury Real Estate): +25 points.
- **Mid-Ticket Local Services** (Plumbing, Auto Repair, Accounting, Landscaping, Fitness/Wellness): +18 points.
- **General Commercial Businesses**: +12 points.
- **Low-Ticket / Commodity**: +5 points.

### Dimension 3: Research Signals & Market Gaps (0 to 20 Points)
- **Competitor Gap Identified** (Competitors offer online booking / portfolio that lead lacks): +10 points.
- **Pain Points Discovered** (Explicit customer complaints about booking or outdated phone-only contact): +6 points.
- **Expansion / Hiring Signals**: +4 points.

### Dimension 4: Conversion & Booking Opportunity (0 to 20 Points)
- **Appointment-Driven Business with No Booking CTA**: +12 points.
- **No Clear Form or Quote Capture**: +8 points.
- **Weak Social Proof Display** despite high Google reviews: +5 points.

### Dimension 5: Data Completeness & Outreach Viability (0 to 10 Points)
- **Direct Email Address Verified**: +5 points.
- **Decision Maker Name or Role Identified**: +3 points.
- **Phone & Physical City Verified**: +2 points.

---

## 3. Tier Classification

| Score Range | Priority Tier | Visual Badge | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **75 – 100** | High Opportunity | 🔥 High | Generate mockup immediately; send personalized framework email. |
| **50 – 74** | Medium Opportunity | ⚡ Medium | Conduct research; audit site; queue for review. |
| **0 – 49** | Low Opportunity | ○ Low | Archive, nurture, or revisit in 6 months. |

---

## 4. Disqualification Rules (Hard Red Flags)
- Lead marked `do_not_contact` or suppressed email.
- Permanently closed business.
- Incompatible vertical (national enterprise chains with centralized IT, government bodies).
